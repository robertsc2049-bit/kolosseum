      Programme builder

      New programme

      Build ordered training blocks. Each block contains weeks, each week contains sessions, and each session contains 1-12 registry-backed exercises, which can be grouped into supersets or circuits and marked as warm-up, working, or cool-down. Repetitions may be fixed or a range. Loading may use percentage of 1RM, fixed weight, bodyweight, or RPE. Percentage prescriptions resolve from the athlete profile when assigned. Each exercise may instead be prescribed by a timed hold or a distance, and reps-based exercises may carry an optional coaching tempo.
