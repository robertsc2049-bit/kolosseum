4. Health information and your consent

  1. 4.1Health and wellbeing information is special category data. We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give when you create your account, and only to provide the Service to you and, if you are connected to a coach, to that coach.
  2. 4.2Recording health information is optional: you choose what to record. You may withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete your health records or your account (see section 9). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it. Features that depend on health information (for example pain carry-forward and readiness check-ins) will not be available without it.
  3. 4.3We do not use your health information to make any diagnosis, and we do not share it with employers, insurers or any other third party except as described in this notice or as required by law.

5. Who can see your information

  1. 5.1Your coach. While you are connected to a coach, that coach can see your training profile, training records and health and wellbeing information, and the messages and videos you exchange. When the connection ends, the coach no longer sees new information. Where a coach copies or uses your data outside the Service for their own business, the coach is responsible for it as an independent controller.
  2. 5.2A head coach or organisation. If your coach works within an organisation that has a head coach, the head coach may see your training records to the same extent as your coach. Gym owners see only the number of their members who use the Service, not who they are or their records, unless you choose to share more.
  3. 5.3Other users. Other athletes do not see your records. Coaches and gyms you contact see the details you send them.
  4. 5.4Our staff. We access personal data only where necessary to run, support and secure the Service, to act on your requests or reports, or to comply with the law.
  5. 5.5Authorities. We may disclose personal data where we are legally required to (for example to HM Revenue & Customs or under a court order), or where necessary to protect someone's vital interests or to prevent or detect crime.
  6. 5.6Business transfer. If our business is sold or reorganised, personal data may be transferred to the new owner, who will be bound by this notice.

7. International transfers

  1. 7.1Where personal data is transferred outside the United Kingdom, we ensure it is protected: transfers to the European Economic Area rely on the UK's adequacy regulations; transfers to the United States rely on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, or otherwise on the International Data Transfer Addendum to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

8. How long we keep it

  1. 8.1We keep your personal data for as long as your account is open.
  2. 8.2If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data from the live Service once we have verified the request, normally within 30 days. Copies in our backups are deleted within 14 days after that.
  3. 8.3We keep records we are required to keep by law for the periods the law requires - for example, accounting and tax records for six years after the end of the financial year to which they relate - and records needed to establish or defend legal claims for up to six years.
  4. 8.4Server logs, which can include IP addresses, are rotated automatically: they are limited to 30 MB for each part of the Service, the oldest entries are overwritten first, and all of them are deleted whenever that part of the Service is updated.

9. Your rights

  1. 9.1Subject to conditions and exceptions set out in the law, you have the right to:
    1. access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
    2. have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed;
    3. have your data erased;
    4. restrict our processing of your data;
    5. receive data you gave us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and have it transmitted to another controller (data portability);
    6. object to processing based on our legitimate interests; and
    7. withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  2. 9.2You can download your data and request deletion of your account directly in the Service. For any other request, contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond within one month, which may be extended by up to two further months for complex requests; we will tell you if that happens. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

10. Rules applied to your sessions

  1. 10.1The Service applies fixed, published rules to the information entered - for example, working out weights from a percentage of a recorded maximum, holding back sessions during a medical stand-down you have recorded, or lightening a session after a low readiness check-in where you or your coach has switched that rule on. Each such rule is shown to you when it applies.
  2. 10.2We do not carry out decision-making based solely on automated processing that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

11. Cookies and storage on your device

  1. 11.1We use only cookies and device storage that are strictly necessary to provide the Service you request: a session cookie to keep you signed in and protect against cross-site request forgery, and browser storage that keeps your preferences and holds session records you log without a signal until they can be sent. These do not require consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003.
  2. 11.2We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

12. Security

  1. 12.1We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls that limit who can see each record, rate limiting, and off-site backups held with restricted access. No system is completely secure; if a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will tell you without undue delay.

13. Age

  1. 13.1The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, please tell us and we will delete it.

14. Complaints

  1. 14.1If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, please complain to us first, using the details in section 1. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay, telling you the outcome.
  2. 14.2You also have the right to complain to the Information Commission (which continues to operate as the ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

15. Changes to this notice

  1. 15.1We will update this notice when our processing changes, and give it a new version number. We will tell you about significant changes by email or in the Service, and ask for your consent again if a change requires it.
